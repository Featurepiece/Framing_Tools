Sync across devices

Synced Store
First-time setup
  1. Make a free account at jsonbin.io.
  2. Copy your Master Key from the API Keys page and paste it above.
  3. Hit Create to make your store, or paste an existing Bin ID.
  4. On any other device you use, enter the same Master Key and Bin ID once.
Your key stays on this device only — it isn't in the app's code.
